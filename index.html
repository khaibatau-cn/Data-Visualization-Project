Beyond the road toll: who really gets hurt on Australian roads

For every road death, dozens more people end up in hospital. Data: BITRE, hospitalised injuries from road crashes, 2011–2021.

Scene 1 · The hook

Road crash hospitalisations are flat, far above the 30% target

39,505 people were hospitalised in 2021, 0.9% above the 2018–20 average.

Scene 3 · The surprise

Nearly half of cyclist injuries involve no collision at all

At least 1 in 5 involve a car or van. About 23% are “other or unspecified”, so the car share is a minimum.

In 2020, cyclists were the only group with more injuries
